Used Car Market Analysis.


Overview

We are team Epsilon (Hendro Iman Pangestu, Fakhri Unggul Indito, Ferdinand Leonardi)

Our user is used-car selling platform listing websites or apps.

We run algorithm to make the best price-based segmentation for each dataset given to us.

This project explores the dynamics of the used car market through statistical analysis, data visualization, and pricing behavior assessment.
The objective is to identify THE PRICE DRIVER that influence vehicle pricing, negotiation tendencies, and market competitiveness across different brands and vehicle conditions.

Our analysis investigates:

Price distribution across vehicle brands and models
Relationship between mileage and selling price
Negotiation patterns among sellers
Most negotiable car brands
Impact of vehicle age on pricing
Statistical association between vehicle brand and negotiability
Mileage categories with the highest negotiation rates

Key Findings
1. Brand and Type of car is the main Price Driver. By running OLS Regression Script on Python we can surely conclude that price fluctuation depend heavily on Brand and Type

2. Mileage and Year Influences Vehicle Condition. A negative correlation exists between mileage and vehicle price, although the strength of this relationship is again varies by brand and model segment.

3. Negotiation Behavior Is Not Random. Statistical testing (Chi-Square Analysis) demonstrates that negotiation likelihood is associated with vehicle brand, suggesting that seller behavior differs across manufacturers.

4. Mileage Segments Affect Negotiation Rates. Vehicles within specific mileage ranges tend to experience higher negotiation activity, revealing potential sweet spots for value-focused buyers.

5. Market Pricing Exhibits High Variability

Only then, we make the best data-proven price-based segmentation for our user
